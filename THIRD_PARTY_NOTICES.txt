BepInEx is copyright its contributors and is distributed under LGPL-2.1.
Project and license information: https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx

The bundled BepInEx.Unity.IL2CPP.dll contains a compatibility adjustment for
Unity 6000.4.1. The corresponding transformation source is included in this
repository as patch-bepinex-load-order.ps1. The generated Unity interop assembly
compatibility adjustment is documented in patch-unity6-interop.ps1.

The game, its assets, and executable are not included in this installer.
